I bent over the mower because the metal plate had caught the light. The engine was cold. Dust sat between the cooling fins, and dried grass was packed along one edge of the housing. The plate carried a model number, a barcode, a QR code, and the small warning that data rates applied. I took out my phone but did not scan it. The garage stayed quiet around the machine. A cable ran from the handle to the engine, and the square code waited beside the serial number.
