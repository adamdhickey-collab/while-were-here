These are small essays about being alive, written mostly on foot.

None of them argue for anything. I have gotten steadily less interested in being
right and steadily more interested in noticing things, which is a poor strategy
for winning discussions and a good one for most of the rest of it.

You can read this from the beginning. You can also open it anywhere, read four
pages, and put it down. It was built to survive both.
