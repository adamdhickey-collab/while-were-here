# Photo selection 01 — the iPhoto export

456 unique stills reviewed (591 files, 135 exact duplicates dropped, 22 videos
reduced to one frame each). Source: `~/Desktop/Photos from iphoto`.

**26 selected. 4 held. The rest out.**

---

## The verdict first

This folder is a travel album. Spain and Portugal in September 2024, with a
smaller Colombia set. It is well shot and there is real material in it, but it
does not solve the problem it was pulled to solve.

The reason is in your own rules. [asset-system.md](asset-system.md) defines
PERSONAL as "family, dogs, the lake, old objects, places with meaning," living
in "Field Notes, closing spreads, the domestic slots," in "Stages I and V,
mostly, the calm ends of the book." A vacation is not the domestic end of a
life. Photographs of Sagrada Família and the Alhambra, however good, read as
places anyone can go. They cannot carry the line the book is actually making,
which is that the person writing it was paying attention to their own ordinary
week.

So almost nothing here enters as PERSONAL. What it does deliver, unexpectedly
and in quantity, is **SPECIMEN, MATERIAL, TEXTURE and FIGURE** — and one essay's
worth of something better than that, below.

**The windfall is Part IV.** *Why Humans Need Pilgrimages* is in the eight-essay
slate, and this folder is unusually well stocked for exactly that essay:
Montserrat, Fátima, Batalha, the Initiation Well at Regaleira, a dozen cathedral
interiors, a mountain switchback path. That is not a coincidence you planned and
it is the single strongest argument for this export.

## Production constraint that shapes everything below

The page is 300 × 300 mm. At 300 dpi that needs **3543 × 3543 px**.

Most of these are 12 MP phone files at 4032 × 2268. Crop one to square and you
have 2268 px, which is **192 dpi at 300 mm**. That is under spec for a
full-bleed square crossover.

The 48 MP files (5712 × 3213) crop square to 3213 px, or **272 dpi**, which is
acceptable on layflat photographic paper.

**Consequence:** treat the 12 MP files as plates, bands, half-pages and cutouts
inside the margins, where they only ever fill 150 to 200 mm and land at 300 dpi
or better. Reserve full-bleed for the 48 MP files and for the four MATERIAL
breaks. Resolution is noted per entry.

## Out, and why

Roughly 400 frames. Not because they are bad photographs.

**Posed at the camera (about 180 frames).** Your global rule is "no one staring
at the camera." This is most of the export.

**Monument documentation (about 120).** Sagrada Família, the Mezquita, the
Alcázar, Batalha, Pena. Competent, but a reader cannot tell them from stock, and
the book's whole claim is first-hand attention.

**Interiors and meals (about 60).** Restaurant tables, food, hotel rooms. These
are the memory of a trip, not evidence of a mechanism.

**Artwork reproductions (about 15).** Banksy, Dalí, the museum paintings, the
gallery pieces. These carry someone else's copyright and your
[asset-sources.md](asset-sources.md) requires provenance on every archival
asset. Not worth the clearance.

## What is still missing

This is the important part. The selection above gives the book its specimens,
its materials and most of Part IV. It gives it almost **no PERSONAL**, and
PERSONAL is what you were originally trying to fill.

Two frames out of 456 qualify: the bulldog at the table, and the man walking
dogs on an empty street. The asset system budgets 10 to 15 personal images and
puts them in Stages I and V, the calm ends. Those slots are still empty.

They will not be filled from a vacation folder. What the book needs, and what
only you can shoot, is roughly this:

1. **The lake, four times, in four weathers.** The back cover already promises
   it: "you can walk around a familiar lake believing almost nothing is
   happening." Right now that lake does not exist as a picture.
2. **The dog, in domestic light, not posed.** You already have two generated
   dog plates. One real one would make them both read differently.
3. **The desk, the kitchen counter, the back step.** Ordinary surfaces at
   ordinary times. Shot from standing height, not styled.
4. **Old objects with wear on them.** A tool, a mug, a key, a boot. These are
   your best SPECIMEN cutouts and there is not one in this folder.
5. **The same view, repeatedly.** A window, a corner, a tree, photographed on
   different days. The book keeps arguing for repeated attention and has no
   evidence of anyone doing it.
6. **One roll of genuinely old family photographs**, scanned, unretouched. Stage
   V wants them and nothing else can stand in.

Six shoots, none of them requiring travel, most of them possible in a week. That
is where the authenticity comes from. Not from Granada.
