# Photo selection 02 — the dated folder

52 stills across five days, Sep 30 to Oct 9 2025. Source:
`~/Desktop/more photos`, organized by date.

**14 selected. 2 blocked on rights. The rest out.**

**Standing rule, confirmed 19 Aug:** everything candid. No group shots, no
portraits, nobody looking at the lens and smiling. This is already the
storyboard's photography rule and it applies to PERSONAL too, which is where the
temptation lives. Applying it strictly cost three of my own first picks: two
couch portraits and a standing shot at the fire pit, all cut below.

Numbers in the labels below are registered in [facts.json](../facts.json) or
marked "do not set yet." Same rule as [photo-selection-01](photo-selection-01.md).

---

## This is the folder that matters

Fifty-two frames, and it does what four hundred and fifty-six could not.

[photo-selection-01](photo-selection-01.md) ended with six shoots the book still
needed. This folder already contains four of them: **the lake**, **the dog in
domestic light**, **ordinary surfaces at ordinary times**, and **a repeated
view**. It is not a trip. It is a fortnight of somebody's actual life, which is
the only thing that can carry the PERSONAL role.

The hit rate is also completely different. The travel folder yielded 26 usable
frames out of 456, about six percent. This one yields 14 out of 52, about
twenty-seven percent. Small, close, unremarkable subjects photographed near home are simply
better raw material for this book than anything with a monument in it.

**One thing it establishes.** The back cover promises "a familiar lake" and
until now that lake did not exist as a picture. It does now.

**B011 · IMG_5212** · moored sailboats, seawall and railing, bright afternoon
Role PLATE · Ordinary Days, full-bleed or wide band · Stage I · 5712 × 3213
- **Crop** Square available at 3213 px, or run it as a wide band. This is one of
  the few frames in either folder with the resolution for a real full-bleed.
- **Color** As shot. Hold the green in the water.
- **Label** `The lake, on a day when nothing was happening.`
- **Why** The back cover's sentence, photographed. That alone earns it.

**B013 · IMG_5215** · the bandshell from across empty grass, lake behind

- **Color** As shot.
- **Label** `Built for an audience. Photographed at nine in the morning.`

**B049 · IMG_5188** · model sailboats on a pond, people on the far bank
Role PLATE · Ordinary Days or the closing spread · Stage I or V · 4032 × 2268

### The dog, and the domestic

**B032 · IMG_5248** · suburban street, a figure crouched with a dog on the
sidewalk in the middle distance, wires, big sky
Role PLATE · Ordinary Days, **opener candidate** · Stage I · 5712 × 3213
- **Crop** Do not crop in. The scale of the empty street against the two small
  figures is the whole picture.
- **Color** As shot.
- **Texture** Global paper only.
- **Label** `Someone stopping, for no reason, on a street with nothing on it.`
- **Why** The strongest frame in this folder and a genuine rival to the cumulus
  for the Essay 01 opener. It is 48 MP, so it can go full-bleed.

**B039 · IMG_5259** · fire pit at dusk, a figure seated in profile watching it,
trees and open sky behind
Role PERSONAL · While We're Here, closing spread · Stage V · 4032 × 2268
- **Crop** Wide. Keep the trees and the sky. The figure should stay small.
- **Color** As shot. Do not lift the shadows.
- **Texture** None. Global paper over a near-black frame turns to mud.
- **Label** Handwritten, short.
- **Why** Dark ground, luminous subject, and nobody performing. The person is
  looking at the fire, which is what makes it a photograph of an evening rather
  than a photograph of a person. Stage V wants exactly this.

**B038 · IMG_5258** · the same fire earlier, figure on a lounger with the dog,
head down
Role PERSONAL · **reserve** for B039 · Stage V · alternate if the spread wants
the dog in it

**B027 · IMG_5241** · a lap, a dog stretched across it in a red harness, a glass
on a side table. No face in frame.
Role PERSONAL · Ordinary Days, domestic slot · Stage I
- **Crop** Keep the whole lap and the side table. The framing is the point: the
  camera is where the person's eyes are, so the reader gets the view rather than
  the portrait.
- **Color** As shot. **Do not retouch.** Indoor white balance and all.
- **Texture** Global paper, and ephemera is allowed here.
- **Why** The domestic frame the first folder was missing, and it passes the
  candid rule by construction because nobody is visible to look at the lens.

**B008 · IMG_5209** · the dog on a grass slope, head turned off-frame, mid-lick
Role PERSONAL · Ordinary Days · Stage I
- **Crop** Cut to the dog and its shadow on the slope.
- **Why** The only dog frame in the folder where the animal is not addressing the
  camera. The parking-lot and sidewalk frames are all portraits and are out.
- **Note** Marginal even so. If the lake shoot produces a dog frame with the
  animal doing something rather than sitting for the lens, replace this.

### Ordinary sky, repeated

**B007 · IMG_5203** · the sun through a break in heavy cloud, warm and dark
Role PLATE · Ordinary Days or a stage turn · Stage I · 4032 × 2268
- **Crop** Band or square. Keep the darkness at the edges.
- **Color** As shot. This is the most striking sky in either folder.
- **Label** **Do not set yet.** Any note about cloud type needs identifying, and
  I am not going to guess a species from a photograph.

**B006 · IMG_5200** · thin cloud against olive-grey, quiet
**B030 · IMG_5245** · high cirrus over blue
**B031 · IMG_5246** · broken cloud, mid-morning
Role PLATE, as a **set of three** · Attention, a contact-sheet spread · Stage I
- **Crop** Identical square crops, gridded, same size, captioned only with time.
- **Color** As shot, no matching. The point is that they differ.
- **Why** This is the "same view, repeatedly" shoot, already done. Three skies
  from three days, set as a grid with nothing but timestamps, is a field
  observation rather than three pictures of weather. It is also the cheapest
  spread in the book to make and one of the most on-argument.

Role PLATE · Attention, near the notice · Stage I · 4032 × 2268

- **Label** `The route, at the hour nobody uses it.`

### Objects and machines

**B001 · IMG_5194** · Jeremy Narby, *Intelligence in Nature*, on a wooden table
Role PERSONAL / EPHEMERA · The Intelligence Outside Your Head, taped inset ·
Stage II · 1290 × 1606
- **Crop** Cut the book out of the table, or leave a margin of wood and tape it
  to the page. Either reads; do not do both.
- **Color** As shot.
- **Texture** Ephemera allowed. One piece of tape, not two.
- **Label** `Narby, 2005. Read before the essay was written, which is the wrong
  way round and is why the essay exists.`
- **Why** The source of an idea, photographed, on the spread where the idea
  lands. Small file, so hold it to about 100 mm, which is the right size for a
  taped object anyway.
- **Rights** Photographing your own copy on your own table is fine. Do not crop
  to the jacket art alone, which would reproduce the cover as artwork.

**B005 · IMG_5199** · engine plate: model number, serial, barcode, QR, and the
words "Data Rates apply"
Role SPECIMEN · The Strange Privilege, or Systems · Stage III · 883 × 597
- **Crop** Square, tight to the plate. It is small, so use it small: 60 to 75 mm.
- **Color** Desaturate to the stage's neutral so it reads as a document.
- **Texture** None.
- **Label** `A lawnmower that would like to talk to your phone.`
- **Why** The book sets its own specimen labels in mono, with codes and numbers,
  and here is a real one made by a manufacturer for a machine. A found specimen
  card. "Data Rates apply" on a mower is the joke and it is not one I wrote.

**B004 · IMG_5197** · the mower itself, from above
Role PLATE · **reserve**, pairs with B005 as object and its label

## Blocked on rights

Both of these are good ideas and neither can be printed as photographed.

**B016 · IMG_5226** · a streaming service's "Today's Top Picks for You," two
films with their promotional artwork
- The interface is one company's and the artwork is other companies'. Printing
  it in a book for sale is a clearance problem, not a grey area.
- **Do this instead.** Redraw it as a FIGURE in `src/layouts/diagrams.mjs`, the
  way the book already draws its diagrams so they inherit stage ink and stay
  vector at 300 mm. A generic recommendation grid, real proportions, no logos,
  no titles, captioned with what the row actually is. It will be better than the
  screenshot because it can be honest about the mechanism.

**B045 · IMG_5184** · a phone screenshot of an "AI Motion Designer" landing page
- Same problem, one company's brand and interface.
- **Do this instead.** Same treatment. The interesting content is the phrase and
  the empty prompt box, and both survive being redrawn.

## What is still missing

Two of the six shoots remain, and they are the two that cannot be improvised.

1. **The lake in other weathers.** You have it bright and busy with boats. The
   back cover's sentence is about a day when almost nothing is happening, so it
   needs at least fog, rain or ice. Same spot, same framing as B011, three more
   times.
2. **Old family photographs**, scanned, unretouched. Stage V still has nothing
   and nothing else can substitute.

Two smaller gaps worth a single afternoon: **worn objects on a plain ground** for
the specimen row, and **a desk or counter at working height**. Neither exists in
either folder.
