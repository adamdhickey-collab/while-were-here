# Photo selection 04 — the whole library, again

672 unique frames across every folder in `~/Desktop/Photos from iphoto`, which
is all three of the earlier passes at once: the top-level iPhoto export
([01](photo-selection-01.md), 456 frames), `more photos`
([02](photo-selection-02.md), 52) and `trips`
([03](photo-selection-03.md), 182). 802 files, 130 duplicates dropped.

**41 selected. 6 blocked on rights. Two fixes to decisions already made.**

Candid rule applies throughout: no group shots, no portraits, nobody looking at
the lens. Two frames below are flagged because they break it and are good
enough to argue about anyway.

Numbers in labels go through [facts.json](../facts.json) or are not set.

---

## Why do it again

The first three passes were each run against a different book. Pass 01 predates
the eight-essay slate. Pass 02 predates Field Notes. None of them were looking
for **material breaks**, for **Part II and Part III**, or for the resolution a
300 mm full bleed actually needs. So this pass reads the same 672 frames
against what the book is now short of, which is:

- Parts II and III have almost no photography. Every essay after *The Secret
  Life of Attention* is unillustrated.
- The MATERIAL register — surface with no edge and no scale cue — has three
  generated images and no real ones.
- Stage III and Stage IV want dark-ground imagery and the book has none.
- Field note 2 is unusable at the size it was given.

## Two fixes

### Field note 2 is not the plate. It is the mower.

`B005 · IMG_5199`, the engine plate with **Data Rates apply**, is **883 × 597**.
That is 50 dpi across a 300 mm page. [Selection 02](photo-selection-02.md) always
said to use it at 60 to 75 mm; the field-note verso is a full bleed, and the two
were never compatible. There is no larger original in any folder.

**`IMG_5197`** · `more photos/Oct 2, 2025` · **3213 × 5712** — the mower itself
from directly above, red on wet concrete, leaves in the gutter beside it.
- **Use** Field note 2 verso, square crop. The plate goes on the recto as a
  small taped inset at its real size, which is the size the joke wants anyway.
- **Why** It is the same object and the same afternoon, at eleven times the
  pixels. And the machine reads better as a machine before you are told it
  would like to talk to your phone.

### Field note 3 cannot be square

`IMG_5248` is a 5712 × 3213 frame in which two figures are small and incidental
against an empty street. Cropped to a square that is 1.8× tighter, they become
a couple facing the camera and smiling, which is precisely what the candid rule
exists to prevent. The frame did not change; the crop changed what it is.

`IMG_5249` is the same setup one frame later and has the same problem.

**Resolved 19 Aug 2026: it runs as a wide band.** The field-note page type
gained a `band` variant, the square crop was thrown away, and the photograph is
back at its native 5712 x 3213, bleeding left and right at 484 dpi with the page
above and below it.

That restores the picture: an empty street, wires, a school, a big sky, and two
small figures on the sidewalk. **The person is still facing the camera.** What
changed is that they are now small enough that the frame is about the street,
which is the reading the candid rule was protecting. If that is still too much,
the answer is a different frame rather than a different crop.

## Blocked on rights

The Renwick's *Wonder* exhibition, May 2016. Six frames, all excellent, all
photographs of contemporary sculpture by living or recently living artists.

| Frame | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| P1050270, P1050271 | Patrick Dougherty's woven-branch structures |
| P1050272, P1050277 | Gabriel Dawe's thread spectra |
| P1050283 | a suspended rod installation |
| P1050277 | Tara Donovan's index-card towers |

`P1050272` is the closest thing in the library to what the art direction means
by *expanded perception, not psychedelia* — a real spectrum thrown across a
white gallery, with people standing in it. It would be the best image in Part
IV. **Photographing a copyrighted three-dimensional work and printing it in a
book for sale needs the artist's permission**, and this book has already decided
to track provenance from the start rather than retrofit it. Either write to the
artists or leave the whole exhibition out. Do not use it and hope.

`P1050330`, the Audubon flamingo hanging in a gallery, is a different case: the
print is public domain and the photograph is Adam's. Usable with a real credit.

## What is still missing after three folders and 672 frames

Nothing in the library serves these, and no amount of re-reviewing will change
that. They have to be shot.

- **A kitchen table with a laptop on it while domestic life happens around it.**
  *The Strange Privilege* opens on this and there is no frame of it.
- **The dog under the desk while extraordinary work happens above.** Same essay,
  and the one image in the whole storyboard that is purely his to take.
- **Before-time objects.** A paper map, a phone book, a printed photograph, an
  answering machine. `IMG_4277` is the closest and it is the Berlin Wall.
- **A desire path.** The single most literal image *Systems Nobody Designed*
  wants, and there is not one in 672 frames. It is a five-minute walk.
- **A repeated view across four seasons.** The generated set has one; a real one
  would carry more, and it costs a year, so it starts now or not at all.

## Field note 4 was the wrong frame — corrected 19 Aug 2026

`B039 · IMG_5259` was placed as the fire-pit verso on the strength of this
description in [photo-selection-02](photo-selection-02.md):

> The person is looking at the fire, which is what makes it a photograph of an
> evening rather than a photograph of a person.

**She is not.** In that frame she is looking straight into the lens, lit from
below, and cropped to a full-bleed square she fills a large part of the page. It
is the same failure field note 3 had, and worse, because the face is larger and
lit. The description was written from memory of the evening rather than from the
frame.

**`B038 · IMG_5258` is the photograph that description fits.** The deck, the
fire lit in its bowl, a figure seated in profile on a lounger with the dog
beside her, on the phone, looking away. Trees and a fence behind, split logs
stacked at the right. Nobody looking at the lens.

It runs as a wide band, like field note 3, at 341 dpi across a 300 mm page. Both
personal field notes now use the same treatment, which is an accident of two
separate corrections and is better than what either of them replaced.

**The lesson, recorded because it will recur:** a selection note written from
memory is not a caption. Check the description against the frame before the
frame goes on a page.

## The library is not on this machine — 21 Aug 2026

**This document selected 41 frames. Three of them are in the book.** The other
thirty-eight are not, and the reason is not editorial: `~/Desktop/Photos from
iphoto` does not exist on the machine the book is now being made on. It was on
the old laptop.

The three that made it — `IMG_5212` (field note 1), `IMG_5248` (field note 3),
`IMG_5258` (field note 4) — were placed before the switch.

**Why this matters more than any other outstanding item.** Everything selected
here runs 3024–5712 px. The Facebook archive that the 20–21 August passes worked
from tops out at 1280 px, which is 81 dpi on a 300 mm full bleed. The same slot
filled from this library would be 270 dpi or better. The book has been accepting
soft openers because the sharp originals were on another computer.

It also fills gaps the Facebook archive cannot. Read the "What is still missing"
section above: MATERIAL surfaces, dark-ground imagery for stages III and IV, and
photography for Parts II and III. The Facebook archive has almost none of that.
This document already found it and argued for each frame.

**Recovery.** `~/Pictures/Photos Library.photoslibrary` exists on this machine
and is live, but macOS TCC refuses process access to it — `Operation not
permitted`, which is expected and not a fault. Reading Apple's internal
`originals/` tree is not a real option either: the files sit under UUID
directories and the filename mapping lives in `database/Photos.sqlite`.

The supported path is an export from Photos.app to a plain folder, **File →
Export → Export Unmodified Original**, with File Naming set to *Use File Name*
so the `IMG_####` names in this document match. Any tooling in this repo then
works unchanged.

Until that folder exists, treat the thirty-eight entries above as selected and
blocked, not as rejected.
