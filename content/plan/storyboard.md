# Visual storyboard

The per-essay visual plan. This is the shot list at the level of *editorial
intent* — `content/images.json` is where an entry becomes a real asset with an
ID, filename and status. Nothing here is committed to layout until it moves into
an essay's `spreads:` frontmatter.

> **Open conflict — part numbering.** The storyboard below numbers the sections
> III / IV plus a coda; the original book brief and `content/contents.json` use
> I Look Again · II What Are We? · III The World Is Changing · IV While We’re
> Here. The storyboard also introduces two structures the brief did not have:
> **Field Notes** (personal interludes) and an essay, **Recovery Is More Than
> Stopping**. Needs one decision before Part II is laid out. Everything below is
> recorded as written.

---

## Global image rules

**Photography** — documentary, natural, quiet. No one staring at the camera.
Avoid over-perfect AI polish. Strong negative space. Occasional blur, grain, odd
crop. Ordinary life elevated through attention.

**Illustration** — field-note / scientific / archival. Imperfect. Cream paper,
charcoal, rust, cobalt, moss. No corporate infographic style, no generic vector
characters.

**Personal imagery** — preserve imperfection. Don’t over-retouch. Let old photos
feel old. Use sparingly so they carry weight.

**Typography-only spreads** — treat these as images in their own right. Not every
philosophical point needs an illustration.

### Asset estimate for a 120–140 pp book

| Kind | Count |
| --- | --- |
| Photographs | 45–55 |
| Illustrations / diagrams | 25–30 |
| Personal / archive | 10–15 |
| Typography-only compositions | 10–15 |
| **Total visual moments** | **90–110** |

Many are simple, or reused as crops and details.

## Field notes — personal interludes

These should visually interrupt the formal essay structure. They are a **new
spread type** and will need their own renderer and CSS.

### FIELD NOTE 01 — Around the Lake
Four to six small images from one walk: shoes, water, dog, bird, cracks in
pavement, changing light. Laid out as a contact sheet or observational journal.
**No hero image.**

### FIELD NOTE 02 — Kylie
One or two genuine photographs, a great deal of blank space, perhaps one small
associated object. **No AI imagery** except possibly a subtle supporting texture.
This has to feel real.

### FIELD NOTE 03 — Making Things
Sketch · Figma frame · rough prototype · final product · AI prompt fragment ·
output evolution. More process-oriented and graphic. Brings the design work into
the book without letting it become a portfolio.

### FIELD NOTE 04 — Home
Ordinary domestic detail: table, shoes by the door, kitchen light, dog in the
hallway, objects only family would recognise. Potentially the most emotionally
resonant spread in the book, precisely because nothing in it is dramatic.
